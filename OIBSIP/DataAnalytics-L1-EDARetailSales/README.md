# Retail Sales Data - Exploratory Data Analysis

## Objective
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify sales trends, customer demographics, product performance, and actionable business insights.

## Tech Stack
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Project Features
- Initial dataset inspection
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly and quarterly sales trend analysis
- Customer demographics analysis
- Top-selling products analysis
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation heatmap
- Revenue by customer age group
- Business recommendations based on findings

## Key Insights
- Customers aged **46–60** generated the highest revenue.
- Best-selling products were identified for better inventory management.
- High-revenue product categories were identified.
- Sales trends can be used for demand forecasting and inventory planning.

## Business Recommendations
- Target customers aged **46–60** with personalized marketing campaigns.
- Ensure sufficient inventory for best-selling products.
- Increase focus on high-performing product categories.
- Use monthly and quarterly sales trends for demand forecasting.
- Introduce more products targeted at the **46–60** age group to increase customer engagement and revenue.
